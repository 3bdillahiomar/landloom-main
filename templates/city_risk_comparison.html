About Somalia Municipality Risk Dashboard

The Somalia Municipality Risk Dashboard is an interactive Geographic Information System (GIS) designed to support land administration, cadastral management, and spatial planning in Somalia. The platform integrates land parcels, administrative boundaries, buildings, roads, landmarks, and other spatial datasets into a single web-based dashboard for visualization and analysis.

City Risk Comparison

The City Risk Comparison feature allows users to compare multiple municipalities in Somalia based on various risk indicators, including natural hazards, population density, and infrastructure vulnerability. The full comparison is under development and will be available in future releases of the dashboard.

An interactive GIS platform that transforms fragmented geospatial data into actionable risk information, enabling evidence-based municipal planning, disaster preparedness, and resilient infrastructure investment.

Through collaborative leadership, the Nagaad Project is building resilient, sustainable cities that serve all communities.
