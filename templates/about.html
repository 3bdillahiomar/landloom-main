About Somalia Municipality Risk Dashboard

The Somalia Municipality Risk Dashboard is an interactive Geographic Information System (GIS) designed to support land administration, cadastral management, and spatial planning in Somalia. The platform integrates land parcels, administrative boundaries, buildings, roads, landmarks, and other spatial datasets into a single web-based dashboard for visualization and analysis.

Purpose

The primary purpose of the Somalia Municipality Risk Dashboard is to improve access to reliable geospatial information for land administration, planning, and decision-making. It provides government agencies, planners, surveyors, researchers, and other stakeholders with tools to visualize land information, monitor land use, and support sustainable development.

Key Features

  • Interactive land parcel visualization.
  • Administrative boundary mapping.
  • Buildings, roads, and landmark datasets.
  • Hazard and exposure mapping
  • Dynamic map legend and information panels.
  • Searchable parcel records.
  • Scalable and modern web-based GIS dashboard.

Technology Stack

  • Django (GeoDjango)
  • Django REST Framework
  • PostgreSQL & PostGIS
  • Leaflet.js
  • Bootstrap 5
  • Chart.js
  • DataTables
